How a tip works

  1. Tap the Monero sign under a note or on a profile and pick an amount. Your own wallet app (Unstoppable, monfluo, Cake …) opens with the payment.
  2. The recipient has registered their address and private view key with a watcher. The view key lets it see incoming payments; it can never spend them.
  3. The watcher publishes a signed receipt on Nostr, and the tip shows under the note.

The default watcher is watcher.monostr.com. You can also run your own: a personal watcher on a Raspberry Pi or small server keeps your view key at home, or host a watcher with your own Monero node. Tips can be anonymous.

Everything else you expect

  • Feed, threads, profiles with posts and replies; like, repost, quote, reply
  • Private messages (NIP-17), private bookmarks and mutes, picture uploads, search, notifications
  • Sign in with Amber or a key; light and dark; English, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Turkish

Beta: tips are real Monero on mainnet. Start with small amounts, and check the address you register for receiving. Found a bug or missing something? Open an issue or mention monostr on Nostr.
