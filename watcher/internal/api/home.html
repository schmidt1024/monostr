monostr watcher

Monero tips for Nostr, made visible.

This server is a watcher for the monostr app. It has no website to browse: the app talks to it over a small HTTP API.

What it does

To receive tips, you register your Monero address and your private view key here. The watcher sees your incoming payments and publishes signed tip receipts on Nostr, so everyone sees the tip under the note.

A view key can only see incoming payments. It cannot spend anything. You can unregister at any time, and you can run your own watcher instead.
